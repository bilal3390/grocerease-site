Home Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what data the GrocerEase app ("the app", "we") collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. GrocerEase is developed by BitBrood.

Summary

  • You can use the app without an account. Your data then stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, we store your email, name and profile picture, plus the product prices and barcodes you save, so they can be backed up and synced.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Data we collect

Data stored only on your phone

  • Price history: product names, the prices you enter, and the date and time of each price.
  • Barcode names: which product name belongs to which barcode.
  • Current bill: the items, quantities and prices on today's bill. The bill is never uploaded.

Data stored in your account (only if you sign in with Google)

  • Account details from Google: your email address, name and profile picture URL, and a user ID. We never receive your Google password.
  • Price history and barcode names as described above, linked to your user ID.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. Camera images are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

How we use your data

  • To show your past prices for a product and build your bill.
  • To back up your price history and sync it between your phones when you are signed in.
  • To identify you when you sign in with Google.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Services we use

  • Google Sign-In to sign you in. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase to store accounts and synced price history. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each user can only access their own data. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Open Food Facts to look up a product's name from its barcode. Only the barcode number is sent. No personal data is sent with it. See the Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Signing out removes your price history from that phone. It stays in your account.
  • Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. To delete your account and all synced data, email muhamadbilalzulfiqar@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with. We delete the account and its data within 30 days.
  • You can also remove GrocerEase's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

GrocerEase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
