What it does

GrocerEase is an Android app that remembers what you paid for your groceries. Scan a product's barcode at the shop and the app shows the last price you paid, the lowest and highest price, and how many times you have bought it. Confirm the price and the item goes onto today's bill.

1. ScanPoint the camera at any product barcode (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC).
2. Check the priceSee the last price and your price history for that product.
3. Build your billConfirm the price and it lands on today's bill, ready to share.

Sign in with Google (optional)

You can use GrocerEase without an account; everything is then saved on your phone only. If you sign in with Google, your price history is backed up and synced across your phones. GrocerEase only receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and never your Google password.

Read the Privacy Policy for details on what is stored and how to delete it.
